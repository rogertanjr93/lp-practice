Rochelle Reyes is a Los Angeles-based real estate agent specializing in residential and luxury properties throughout Los Angeles and surrounding communities. She works with first-time buyers, growing families, investors, and homeowners looking to sell.

Rochelle believes that real estate should feel personal and straightforward. She takes the time to understand each client's goals, preferences, and priorities so she can provide guidance tailored to their needs.

Known for her responsive communication and attention to detail, Rochelle helps clients navigate every stage of the real estate process—from exploring properties and preparing offers to negotiating and closing.

Outside of real estate, Rochelle enjoys discovering new restaurants, exploring local neighborhoods, and spending time with family and friends.

Roger Tan

More About Roger +

As the son of a foreign home goods importer, my journey into real estate started early. Along with my father, I took inventory purchases and sales on global trips. I was taught the value of planning, how to negotiate, and how to "collect" opulent hotel bathroom accessories at a young age. Coming home was the highlight of my years of travel, more so than the amazing food, varied scenery, and encounters with different cultures. I would always feel safe, at ease, and like I knew everyone when I opened the front door.

These factors make it crucial for me to assist my clients in finding the ideal home, considering their requirements. For example, it may be a single person's first home, a couple's first home together, a place where parents will raise their children, the neighborhood where their kids will go to school, a potential home for their parents during lengthy visits, and undoubtedly one of their most significant financial decisions.
